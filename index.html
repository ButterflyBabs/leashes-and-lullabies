Meet Mayleen

Hi! I'm Mayleen. I'm 14 and will be 15 soon. I'm the oldest of five kids, with younger siblings ranging from ages 2–8, so helping with little ones has always been a natural part of my life.

Yes. Mayleen is CPR certified and babysitting certified.

No. Pet sitting is provided through 30–60 minute visits and check-ins only.

She may administer some routine medications, but she does not give injections. Medication needs should be discussed in advance.
